How it works

From booking to mailbox in four steps

Everything is handled through the customer portal, so you always know where your campaign stands.

  1. Register

    Create a free customer account with your company details. It takes about a minute.

  2. Book a campaign

    Tell us the material, the area, the number of households and the delivery week.

  3. We confirm the plan

    We check the routes, send you a quote and agree when the printed material reaches us.

  4. Delivered and reported

    Our carriers deliver during the agreed week. You see the status change to Delivered in the portal.

About us

A local delivery company that knows every street

Kangarooposti is a delivery company based in the Rauma and Eurajoki area. Our carriers deliver newspapers and letters here every morning, so we know the routes, the mailboxes and the apartment buildings.

The name comes from the kangaroo's pouch. We carry your message safely and bring it all the way to the door.
